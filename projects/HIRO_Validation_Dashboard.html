Key Takeaways
✅
Validation is the promotion gateNo model reaches KServe without clearing its accuracy threshold — quality is enforced, not advisory.
📊
Five metrics, one decisionAccuracy gates promotion; F1, AUC, precision & recall provide provenance and cross-version comparison.
🖼️
Every run keeps its evidenceConfusion matrix, ROC, PR and calibration artifacts are logged to MLflow alongside the metrics.
🚦
Coverage is the KPI to watch30% of the registry is unscored and blocked — raising validation coverage directly unblocks production readiness.

Quality Gate Thresholds
GateScore rangeAction
Excellent≥ 0.90Auto-promote to serving
Good0.80 – 0.90Promote with review
Fair0.70 – 0.80Hold — manual approval
Poor< 0.70Block — retrain required

Primary gate metric: accuracy, with F1 as a tie-breaker for imbalanced classes. AUC, precision, and recall are recorded for full provenance and cross-version comparison.

No-metrics rule: a model with no validation metrics on file is treated as ungated and is never auto-promoted — it stays out of production until scored.

Model Provenance — Artifacts Stored per Model

Every model carries its full lineage in MLflow (s3://mlflow/<run>/artifacts): the serialized model plus the environment and evidence needed to reproduce and validate it.
